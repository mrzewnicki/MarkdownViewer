VISUAL STYLE — POST-APOCALYPTIC CINEMATIC REALISM

Create a cinematic post-apocalyptic scene in a highly detailed, realistic game concept-art aesthetic.

STYLE:
- photorealistic environmental concept art with subtle painterly qualities
- grounded, believable real-world architecture, vehicles, equipment and materials
- realistic proportions and physically plausible construction
- detailed environmental storytelling
- serious, mature, grounded post-apocalyptic atmosphere
- no fantasy aesthetics, no exaggerated sci-fi design
- the world should feel like a believable place that existed before the collapse

ENVIRONMENT:
- abandoned or partially reclaimed urban environments
- decaying concrete, exposed brick, rusted metal, broken glass and weathered wood
- collapsed infrastructure, damaged roads, abandoned vehicles and improvised structures
- vegetation gradually reclaiming civilization
- tall grass, weeds, ivy, moss, bushes and trees growing through human structures
- signs of previous human activity everywhere: furniture, containers, tools, barricades, cables, scaffolding, graffiti, debris
- strong contrast between old civilization and recovering nature

LIGHTING:
- cinematic natural lighting
- strong directional light
- atmospheric haze and volumetric light
- visible light rays when appropriate
- deep shadows with important details still subtly visible
- realistic global illumination
- carefully controlled highlights
- dramatic silhouettes
- dusk, dawn, overcast daylight or low-light conditions depending on the scene
- practical light sources should strongly affect the environment at night

COLOR PALETTE:
- desaturated natural colors
- muted greens, dirty blues, grey concrete, brown earth and oxidized metal
- occasional warm orange/yellow light sources
- limited but intentional color accents
- slightly cool overall atmosphere
- avoid highly saturated colors
- avoid clean digital-looking colors

COMPOSITION:
- cinematic widescreen composition
- strong foreground, middle ground and background separation
- environmental storytelling through composition
- large architectural shapes combined with smaller human elements
- characters, when present, should provide scale rather than dominate the image
- natural leading lines created by roads, buildings, cables, vehicles and light
- believable camera perspective
- slightly low or eye-level cinematic camera placement
- depth created through atmospheric perspective

DETAIL:
- highly detailed surfaces
- realistic dirt, grime, rust, water stains, cracks, peeling paint and weathering
- believable vegetation density
- small environmental details that suggest how people survive
- realistic clothing, weapons, tools and equipment
- subtle imperfections everywhere
- avoid pristine surfaces unless specifically requested

ATMOSPHERE:
- melancholic
- tense
- lonely
- dangerous but not constantly chaotic
- the world should feel quiet, abandoned and lived-in at the same time
- emphasize the passage of time
- suggest a story without explicitly explaining it

CINEMATIC QUALITY:
- AAA post-apocalyptic video game loading-screen quality
- cinematic environmental concept art
- realistic depth of field used sparingly
- subtle filmic contrast
- atmospheric perspective
- realistic lens behavior
- restrained bloom
- subtle film grain
- high visual fidelity

AVOID:
- cartoon style
- anime
- comic-book aesthetics
- exaggerated fantasy
- excessive cyberpunk
- neon colors
- overly clean environments
- generic stock-photo appearance
- excessive HDR
- oversaturated colors
- plastic-looking materials
- perfectly symmetrical compositions
- overly dramatic fantasy ruins
- excessive visual clutter
- characters posing like action heroes
